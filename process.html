How we work

The Process — how Rainmaker plans your wedding in Kerala and abroad

Your vision, then elevated: from the first conversation to the final farewell.

  • The Curtain Raiser

    It starts with you. Our Curtain Raiser questionnaire asks the questions that shape everything: your dream wedding in three words, your personal style, the colours you love, the “wow” moment you’re imagining, and your wedding-day hashtag. Your answers are the concept; everything after is about elevating it.

  • Destination & Venue

    Finalising the destination, the resort and venues, and room allocation, then marking and layouting every space, from structural plans to event-wise movement and seating.

  • Theme & Design

    This is where your idea becomes a world: a Thrissur Pooram, a Baroque Ball, a Night City. A theme for each event, with floral and colour palettes, 3D designing, venue and collateral design, floral installations, tablescapes and illumination.

  • Guests & Hospitality

    E-invites, physical and box invites, RSVP and guest lists, travel and accommodation rosters, airport and hotel transfers, help desks and theme-based guest welcomes.

  • The Celebration

    Haldi, mehendi, sangeet, the wedding and reception, with ceremonial planning, custom grand entries, entertainment design, dining experiences and bar management.

  • The Keepsakes

    Storybooks, coffee-table and candid photo books, wedding magazines, concept videos, trailers, highlights and live webcasts, so the celebration lives on.
