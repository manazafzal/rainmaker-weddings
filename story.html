About Rainmaker

Our Story — Rainmaker Weddings, destination wedding planners in Kochi since 2011

Born in Kochi in 2011. Raised across oceans, from Kerala’s backwaters to Phuket, Bali and the UAE.

Based out of Kochi, Kerala, Rainmaker Signature Experiences was born in 2011 out of the passion to curate unique and luxurious experiential weddings and social events.

We have been successful with organising destination weddings and socials in Thailand, the UAE, Bali and across India in Udaipur, Mysore, Mangalore and Goa.

We have also been inducted into the Economic Times – Panache Wedding A Lister 2021 category in India, and recognised and awarded for our work by The International Convention of the Wedding Fraternity and WOW Awards Asia.

We are also associated with the crème de la crème of the international wedding industry through the Destination Wedding Planner Congress International and The Wedding Council EEMA.

“We truly believe that great power brings in greater responsibility. Our journey has just commenced and we are all buckled up for the roller coaster rides ahead.”

How we think

Your vision, then elevated.

Every Rainmaker celebration begins with the couple’s own idea: a place, a memory, a mood, a word. We don’t replace it with ours. We take that concept and elevate it into something extraordinary, a signature experience that could only belong to you.

That is how a wedding became a Thrissur Pooram, a reception became a Baroque Ball, and a Sangeet became a neon Night City.
